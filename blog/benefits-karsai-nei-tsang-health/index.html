Karsai Nei Tsang in Costa Rica: Bodywork & Men's Wellness

Karsai Nei Tsang is a bodywork practice inspired by Chi Nei Tsang and Taoist tradition, with special attention to the abdomen, pelvis, and body awareness. Its slow and mindful approach invites the release of tension and encourages a deeper connection with the body's sensations.

A Body Connection Experience

More than a conventional massage, Karsai Nei Tsang offers an experience of presence and body exploration. Each session is provided professionally, with respect for each person's comfort, boundaries, and preferences.

Karsai Nei Tsang & Tantric Massage

In Costa Rica, this technique can be integrated with Tantric Massage to create a more complete experience, combining mindful bodywork, breathing, presence, and sensory exploration.

It is an option for those looking to disconnect from their daily routine, dedicate time to their body, and discover a different way of experiencing massage in a private, peaceful, and respectful environment.
