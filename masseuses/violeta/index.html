Philosophy

The skin holds memories, changes, lessons, and emotions. That is why I see body art as a form of expression capable of transforming each experience into a unique symbol.
My work is about listening, feeling, and interpreting. I do not simply seek to create a sensory experience through the skin, but to leave a lasting mark that connects with the personality, story, and intention of each person who chooses to experience it. THE PHILOSOPHY OF THE SENSORY SELF.
